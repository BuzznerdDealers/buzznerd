November 8–11, San Antonio, Texas. Join us at North America's premier event for used truck professionals! The UTA Annual Convention, anchored by the Used Truck Association and leading industry collaborators, promises an unmatched experience. Here's what's revving up:

- Voices of the industry — speakers who drive the industry forward
- Gear-up workshops — hands-on, truck-focused sessions
- Power networking with like-minded professionals
- Texas-sized celebrations and culinary pit stops
- San Antonio vibes — a city where history meets horsepower

Embrace the momentum of this year's convention, where the spirit of Texas meets the pulse of the used-truck industry. We'll be in booth 413 — stop by and say howdy!
