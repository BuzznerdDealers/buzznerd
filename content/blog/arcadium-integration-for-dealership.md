Our Arcadium Integration connects your dealership website directly to Arcadium, the trucking industry's leading CRM and inventory management platform. The result: real-time inventory visibility, instant lead tracking, and faster follow-ups, all within one connected ecosystem.

## How the Integration Works

### 1. Real-Time Inventory Sync

Our integration automatically syncs listings from Arcadium to your Buzznerd-built website, updating unit details, photos, and specifications in real time. This eliminates manual uploads, minimizes errors, and ensures customers always see what's truly available — saving your team hours every week and reducing confusion for buyers.

### 2. Lead Capture That Goes Straight to Arcadium

Every form submission from your website — sales inquiries, financing requests, and service forms — flows directly into your Arcadium CRM. No more downloading spreadsheets or manually entering lead data. Your sales team can respond faster, track engagement, and measure outcomes directly inside Arcadium.

### 3. Marketing & Newsletter Automation

For dealerships using marketing tools such as Mailchimp, this integration can also route signups from your website's forms directly into your marketing lists — the perfect way to nurture new leads while keeping your CRM and marketing systems aligned.

## Why This Matters

In the trucking industry, timing and trust are everything. When your website, CRM, and marketing tools all speak the same language, your dealership gains a major advantage.

- Accuracy builds confidence — customers see the latest inventory without you lifting a finger.
- Speed drives sales — leads move from inquiry to CRM automatically, so your team can act immediately.
- Automation creates alignment — marketing, sales, and leadership all operate from the same source of truth.
