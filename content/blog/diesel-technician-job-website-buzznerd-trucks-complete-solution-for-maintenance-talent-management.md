_A full job platform for diesel technicians: alerts, searching, resume management, and a manager dashboard._

![Diesel Technician Job Website — A Complete Solution for Maintenance Talent Management](https://d1jj54vtfkx4yj.cloudfront.net/efs/wp/domains/www.buzznerd.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/05/Screen-Shot-2021-07-19-at-11.07.17-PM-1024x426.png)

Buzznerd Trucks recently launched a new job platform geared toward diesel technicians and related fields. Our client, Maintenance Talent Management, set us out with requirements not only for a complete job-management back-end system, but front-end requirements for job searches, applicant submittals, and a special requirement that specific job openings be pulled into their family of websites appropriately. A more comprehensive set of platform features follows.

### For the Job Applicant

- Job alerts
- Job searching and filters
- Resume submission forms
- Candidate dashboard with resume management

### For the Job Manager

- Job and applicant dashboard
- Job editing tools
- Multi-job manager capable
