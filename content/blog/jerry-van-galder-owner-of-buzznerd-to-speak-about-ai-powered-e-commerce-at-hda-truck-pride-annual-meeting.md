_Strategies to grow your customer base, build loyalty, and increase revenue through e-commerce._

![Jerry Van Galder to Speak About AI-Powered E-Commerce at HDA Truck Pride](https://d1jj54vtfkx4yj.cloudfront.net/efs/wp/domains/www.buzznerd.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/04/admin-ajax.png)

Jerry Van Galder, owner of Buzznerd, will speak at the HDA Truck Pride annual meeting, taking place April 12–16 in Aurora, Colorado — an event expected to draw many trucking-industry professionals.

Van Galder's talk will focus on e-commerce and how businesses can use it to increase their customer base, build loyalty, and grow revenue, offering insights and strategies for optimizing operations and reaching a larger audience. With over 20 years in the e-commerce industry, he's well-versed in helping businesses succeed through innovative AI-powered solutions.

> AI-powered e-commerce has become a critical part of doing business in the modern world, and I look forward to sharing strategies that can help trucking companies grow their customer base, build loyalty, and increase revenue. — Jerry Van Galder

Van Galder will also man a booth throughout the conference, where attendees can stop by to ask questions and learn more about Buzznerd's AI-powered e-commerce and website solutions.
