_Our fourth consecutive appearance at the Karmak Conference & Expo — St. Louis Union Station._

![Back for More! Buzznerd Returns to Karmak for a 4th Year](https://d1jj54vtfkx4yj.cloudfront.net/efs/wp/domains/www.buzznerd.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/02/Karmak-logo-white-background-1-2.png)

The countdown is on! We're thrilled to announce our fourth consecutive appearance at the renowned Karmak Conference & Expo. This year's event takes place at the historic St. Louis Union Station from October 1–3, 2024, and we wouldn't miss it for the world.

### Location Details

- Venue: St. Louis Union Station
- Address: 1820 Market Street, St. Louis, MO 63103
- Dates: October 1–3, 2024

### Why We Keep Coming Back

Our commitment to the Karmak community runs deep. Each year we're energized by the industry insights, the cutting-edge trends, and the chance to connect with the people who drive the trucking world forward. This fourth year marks a special milestone, underscoring our dedication to the Karmak ecosystem and the innovation it fosters.

### What To Expect

- Buzznerd in action — see how our AI-powered e-commerce solutions integrate with Karmak
- The future of trucking e-commerce, whether you're a seasoned pro or new to Buzznerd
- Let's talk shop — our team is eager to discuss your challenges and explore solutions

Here's to another year of innovation, partnership, and pushing the boundaries of what's possible in the trucking industry. See you in St. Louis!
