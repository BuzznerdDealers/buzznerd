_Our first year at the ATD Expo — showcasing Facebook Marketplace and rapid-response lead services._

![Buzznerd Trucks Attends First ATD Show!](https://d1jj54vtfkx4yj.cloudfront.net/efs/wp/domains/www.buzznerd.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/05/1500x500.jpg)

The owner of Buzznerd Trucks, Jerry Van Galder, and Success Manager Nikkole Heep will be attending this weekend's 2020 ATD Expo, which will take place at Westgate Las Vegas Resort & Casino. This will be our company's first time attending the 57-year annual show and we are excited to be a part of it.

Buzznerd Trucks is based in Seattle with over 17 years of experience in the heavy truck marketing and technology industries. What sets us apart from the competition is the constant effort to be innovative in a fast-paced and technology-driven world. Our company uses data-driven approaches to get dealers the edge they need to compete with current technological and marketing practices.

The ATD Expo is organized by the American Truck Dealers (ATD), a group under the National Automobile Dealers Association (NADA). Buzznerd Trucks will be one of the exhibitors on hand to offer innovative ways to help dealerships grow their business. This is the perfect avenue for our company to showcase what we can offer to dealers.

Buzznerd's latest service is sharing a dealership's inventory through Facebook Marketplace — a largely untapped platform in the trucking industry. Buyers can browse the dealership's inventory and contact the dealership through Messenger or phone. This is an effective way to acquire leads because Facebook is one of the largest social media platforms, with a huge user base that makes it convenient for buyers to look for what they need. Someone is interested in getting a truck? They search for it in the search bar and come across a dealership's listings. It's that simple!

Aside from posting inventory, we also offer rapid-response services — anyone inquiring about any of the listings will get a quick response from us and be directed to whatever they are looking for.

The Facebook Marketplace service is just one of the many things Buzznerd Trucks is excited to talk to dealers about at the 2020 ATD Expo. Be sure to visit our booth!
