When temperatures drop, small problems turn into big ones fast. A few minutes of
prevention now saves you an expensive tow and a day off the road later.

## 1. Test your batteries

Cold cranking amps drop in freezing weather. Have your batteries load-tested and
replace any that are marginal before they leave you stranded.

## 2. Check your coolant

Make sure your coolant is rated for the lowest temperatures you'll see and that
the mixture is correct. The wrong ratio can freeze and crack a block.

## 3. Fuel and filters

Switch to a winter fuel blend and carry a spare fuel filter. Gelled fuel is one
of the most common cold-weather no-starts.

## 4. Air dryer and brakes

Moisture in your air system freezes lines and locks brakes. Drain tanks daily
and service the air dryer.
