_A first-of-its-kind Karmak-integrated e-commerce platform, powered by AI._

![Buzznerd Launches First AI-Powered E-Commerce Platform in Partnership with Karmak](https://d1jj54vtfkx4yj.cloudfront.net/efs/wp/domains/www.buzznerd.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/02/Karmak-logo-white-background-1-2.png)

Buzznerd, a leader in the heavy-duty truck marketing industry, announced the launch of the first Karmak-integrated e-commerce platform powered by AI technology. This collaboration streamlines the ordering process and provides a more efficient and personalized shopping experience for customers.

With AI technology, Buzznerd's e-commerce platform is able to learn and adapt to customer behavior, providing accurate search results, ranking popular items higher, and generating personalized products based on customer interest. This creates an effortless shopping experience that drives engagement, improves customer satisfaction, and increases sales growth.

Karmak's business management services enhance the e-commerce platform with real-time inventory updates, and the ability for customers to use their existing Karmak accounts. This integration cuts down on paperwork and streamlines the process for companies using Karmak Fusion.

> The strategic partnership with Buzznerd provides an easy-to-use yet very powerful solution that integrates nicely with the Karmak Fusion DMS solution. — Craig Grade, Business Development Director, Karmak

With over 17 years of technology and marketing experience in the heavy-duty trucking sector — and a team of experts from Daimler Trucks, AT&T, Microsoft, and Expedia — Buzznerd offers comprehensive services to help companies take their parts inventory online with AI-powered e-commerce and branding.

Karmak is a leading provider of commercial transport-focused business management solutions, serving over 600 customers, with more than 70% of their clients having worked with them for over a decade. Their Fusion dealer management system provides real-time analytics and covers all aspects of parts management, including accounting, customer pricing, and maintenance tracking.
