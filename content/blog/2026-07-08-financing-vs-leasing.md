One of the first questions we hear is whether to finance or lease. There's no
single right answer — it comes down to how you run your business.

## When financing makes sense

- You keep trucks for the long haul and want to build equity.
- You want no mileage or usage restrictions.
- You'd rather own the asset outright at the end.

## When leasing makes sense

- You want lower monthly payments and predictable costs.
- You like upgrading to newer equipment every few years.
- You'd rather preserve capital for other parts of the business.
