⚡ Questions dealers ask

Buzznerd products, answered

Which product should I start with?

Most dealers start with an AI Website — it's the front door where leads come in. Add AI E-Commerce and the AI Platform whenever you're ready; all three run on the same AI and your live inventory, so nothing gets rebuilt later.

Do the three products work together?

Yes. Every product shares one inventory feed, one customer record and one AI. Website leads flow into the platform, ecommerce orders land in the same place, and channel-to-sale attribution ties it all back to the sold unit — no double entry, no data silos.

What systems does Buzznerd integrate with?

Buzznerd works with Karmak, CDK and major DMS/CRM platforms, pulls inventory and market data from marketplaces like Truck Paper and Machinery Trader, and connects to Google, Meta and Stripe. Custom DMS and ERP integrations are available.

Can I sell parts and products online nationwide?

Yes. AI E-Commerce is a storefront built for dealership complexity — 24/7 nationwide selling with account-based pricing, shipping rules and secure Stripe checkout that closes deals while the counter is dark.

What is the AI Platform?

The AI Platform is the AI under everything Buzznerd builds — answering leads after hours, handing reps a ranked No-Touch CRM morning digest, building listings from a photo with Magic Flow, and tracing every channel to the sold unit.

How do I see how my dealership shows up in AI search?

Run the free AI Presence Score. It scans your dealership and shows how you rank across AEO, AIO, SEO and GEO — the same Magic 8 signals Buzznerd optimizes.
