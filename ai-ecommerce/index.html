⚡ Questions dealers ask

Frequently asked questions.

Can I sell parts online with account-based pricing?

Yes. Buzznerd AI E-Commerce supports account-based pricing and customer-specific workflows, so your online store reflects the pricing each customer sees rather than forcing a single retail price.

How does Buzznerd handle payments, tax, and shipping?

Buzznerd sets up Stripe Payments and Stripe Tax, plus shipping with multiple providers including UPS, FedEx, and USPS, along with shipping and fulfillment rules built for parts and equipment sales.

Does e-commerce connect to my DMS and inventory system?

Yes. Buzznerd connects online products to your DMS and inventory systems — including Karmak and CDK, plus custom DMS and ERP integrations — and syncs product and inventory data so campaigns run on live data.

Can Buzznerd help create the product listings?

Yes. Buzznerd helps generate cleaner product titles, descriptions, categories, tags, FAQs, product details, and search-friendly product pages, so getting products online takes far less manual work.

How does e-commerce connect to marketing?

Buzznerd connects e-commerce products to Google Ads, Meta remarketing, email campaigns, social promotions, monthly parts flyers, product landing pages, and featured category campaigns — all tied to live inventory and product data.
