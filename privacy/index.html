Privacy Policy.

Your privacy matters to us. Here’s what we collect, why, and the choices you have.

Last updated July 10, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Buzznerd, LLC ("Buzznerd," "we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and shares information when you visit buzznerd.com, use our products and services, or otherwise interact with us. By using our site or services, you agree to the practices described here.

1. Information we collect

We collect information you provide directly and information collected automatically as you use our site and services:

  • Information you provide — such as your name, business/dealership name, email address, phone number, website URL, and anything you include in a form, demo request, or message to us.
  • Automatically collected information — such as IP address, browser and device type, pages viewed, referring URLs, and general location, gathered through cookies and similar technologies.
  • Service data — information generated when dealerships use our platform, including inventory, leads, and usage data processed on behalf of our customers.

2. How we use information

We use information to operate, provide, maintain, and improve our products and services; to respond to inquiries and demo requests; to provide customer support; to send relevant communications; to measure and improve marketing and site performance; to detect and prevent fraud or abuse; and to comply with legal obligations.

3. AI Presence Score and website scans

When you request an AI Presence Score or similar scan, we use the name, email, and website URL you provide to generate and deliver your report and to follow up about our services. We may retain scan inputs and results to improve our tools.

4. Cookies and analytics

We use cookies and similar technologies to keep the site functioning, remember preferences, and understand how the site is used. You can control cookies through your browser settings; disabling them may affect site functionality.

5. How we share information

We do not sell your personal information. We share information only with service providers who help us operate our business (such as hosting, analytics, email, and payment providers), with integration partners at your direction, when required by law or to protect our rights, and in connection with a business transfer such as a merger or acquisition.

6. Data retention

We retain information for as long as needed to provide our services, fulfill the purposes described in this policy, and comply with our legal obligations, after which we delete or de-identify it.

7. Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to protect information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Your choices and rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information, and you may opt out of marketing emails at any time using the unsubscribe link or by contacting us. Depending on your location, you may have additional rights under applicable privacy laws.

9. Third-party links

Our site may link to third-party websites and services that we do not control. This policy does not apply to those sites, and we encourage you to review their privacy practices.

10. Children's privacy

Our site and services are intended for businesses and are not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "Last updated" date above, and material changes may be communicated through the site or by email.

Questions about this policy? Contact Buzznerd, LLC at info@buzznerd.com.
