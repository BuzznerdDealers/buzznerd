⚡ Questions dealers ask

Frequently asked questions.

How is Buzznerd marketing different from a normal ad agency?

Buzznerd builds campaigns around your live inventory instead of a generic keyword list. Ads, remarketing, and landing pages reflect what's actually on your lot, and performance is measured all the way to the sale.

Which marketing channels does Buzznerd manage?

Buzznerd manages Google Ads, Meta remarketing, email campaigns, social promotions, and inventory-connected landing pages — all tied to your live inventory and product data.

Can Buzznerd tie marketing spend to actual sales?

Yes. Channel-to-sale attribution connects each lead and sale back to the channel that drove it, so you can see which campaigns produce real deals rather than just clicks.

Do I need a Buzznerd website to use the marketing service?

Marketing works best alongside a Buzznerd website and the AI platform because campaigns run on live inventory data, but we'll discuss options for your current setup during a demo.

How do campaigns stay current with my inventory?

Campaigns are connected to live inventory, parts, and product data, so as units sell or arrive, the ads and landing pages reflect what's actually available.
