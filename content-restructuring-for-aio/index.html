⚡ Questions dealers ask

Frequently asked questions.

What is content restructuring for AIO?

AIO means AI Optimization. Buzznerd restructures your dealership pages — headings, structure, descriptions, and formatting — so content is easy for buyers to read, easy for search engines to rank, and easy for AI answer engines like ChatGPT, Gemini, and Google AI to understand and cite.

Which pages does Buzznerd restructure?

Buzznerd restructures the pages that earn business — sales, service, parts, inventory, location, finance, department, campaign, and landing pages.

Do I have to rewrite everything myself?

No. Buzznerd's team audits, rewrites, and restructures your pages for you, so your staff stays focused on selling while your content gets stronger.

How is this different from normal SEO?

Traditional SEO focuses on ranking in search. Content restructuring for AIO makes pages perform everywhere at once — readable for buyers, structured for search engines, and clear enough for AI platforms to recommend your dealership.

Will restructuring help me show up in AI answers?

Yes. Clean structure, clear headings, and well-organized descriptions make it far more likely that AI answer engines understand your pages and name your dealership when buyers ask. You can also check where you stand with a free AI Presence Score.
