⚡ Questions dealers ask

Buzznerd services, answered

What services does Buzznerd offer?

Four core services: AI-Driven Marketing built on your live inventory, Content Restructuring for AIO so buyers and AI understand your pages, Custom Workflows & Integrations that connect your DMS, CRM and systems, and a free AI Presence Score that shows how AI search sees your dealership.

What is AI-Driven Marketing?

Dealership advertising built around live inventory and measurable performance — Google Ads, Meta remarketing and landing pages tied to real units, so spend follows what's actually on your lot and every campaign is traceable to results.

What is Content Restructuring for AIO?

It takes weak, outdated or thin dealership content and rebuilds it so it performs across traditional search, AI answer engines and customer-facing pages — the same structure that helps AI cite and recommend your dealership.

Can Buzznerd connect to my DMS and CRM?

Yes. We build custom workflows and integrations that connect websites, inventory providers, CRM, DMS, ecommerce and marketing platforms — working with Karmak and CDK plus custom DMS and ERP integrations.

How much does an AI Presence Score cost?

It's free. The AI Presence Score scans your dealership and shows how you rank across AEO, AIO, SEO and GEO in about 30 seconds, then emails you the full report.

Do I need a Buzznerd website to use these services?

No. Buzznerd services can work on your existing site and systems. They run best alongside a Buzznerd AI Website and the AI Platform, but marketing, content, integrations and the AI Presence Score can all start independently.
