⚡ Questions dealers ask

The AI Platform, answered

What is the Buzznerd AI Platform?

The AI under everything Buzznerd builds — the AI Assistant answers leads after hours, No-Touch CRM hands reps a ranked morning digest, Magic Flow builds listings from photos, and attribution traces every channel to the sold unit.

Does the AI Assistant replace my salespeople?

No. It covers inbound questions when no salesperson is available and reads each buyer’s intent and readiness, then hands your team a warm brief. Prices, delivery promises, and trade values always stay human-only.

What is the No-Touch CRM?

A ranked morning digest — not a data-entry CRM. It assembles your hottest follow-ups overnight with replies pre-drafted, so reps act instead of filing. It also plays nice with the CRM you already run.

How fast can Magic Flow list a unit?

About a minute. Snap photos on the lot and Magic Flow builds the title, description, specs, and FAQs. You confirm what it found and set the price — including comp-based price guidance.

Can I see which marketing channel actually sells units?

Yes. Channel-to-sale attribution ties every lead and sale back to the channel that produced it — real sold-unit results, not vanity metrics.
